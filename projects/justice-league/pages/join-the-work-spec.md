# Join the Work Page Specification

- Route: `/join-the-work/`
- Page type: Participation pathway, Documentary Community mode
- Objective: Help visitors compare real current-site participation categories without implying formal membership.
- Audience/intent: Prospective participants, volunteers, committee contributors, presenters/hosts, and partners.
- Primary conversion: Test participation-interest form shell. Secondary: Subscribe.
- Search intent: Join Justice League GLM and volunteer reparations Lansing.
- Proposed title: `Join the Work | Justice League GLM`
- Meta description: `Explore provisional ways to attend, contribute, request a presentation, partner, volunteer, or receive Justice League GLM updates.`
- H1: `Join the Work`
- LCP candidate: Text H1.
- Schema: `WebPage`; no membership schema.
- Canonical/indexability: `/join-the-work/`; prototype noindex, intended index after workflow approval.
- Internal links: Events, Contact, Reparations, Support, newsletter.

## Section hierarchy

| Order | Section | User question | Proof/content | Action |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Participation framing | Is this formal membership? | Explicit `participation, not a membership claim` notice | Compare paths |
| 2 | Ordered pathways | How can I participate? | Meeting, committee, presentation, volunteer, partner, updates | Select interest |
| 3 | Form shell | What information would I send? | Name, email, interest, message; no transmission | Test validation |
| 4 | Expectations | What happens next? | Response timing and owner unresolved | Contact |

## Requirements

- Mobile: Ordered pathways precede the form; controls remain at least practical 44px targets.
- Accessibility/performance: Persistent labels, autocomplete, associated errors, status region; local validation only.
- Unknowns: `[NEEDS CLIENT INPUT: Confirm active pathways, meeting access, committees, membership policy, form owner, response time, consent, and privacy.]`
- Acceptance: No dues/voting/open-meeting claim; no data sent; error and success demo states work by keyboard.
