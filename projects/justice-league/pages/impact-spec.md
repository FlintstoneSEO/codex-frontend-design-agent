- Route: `/impact/`
- Page type: Accountability framework, Evidence mode
- Objective: Show what responsible reporting requires, the dated program evidence currently available, and clear paths to the separate Supporters and Donors records.
- Audience/intent: Donors, partners, grant makers, media, and residents evaluating evidence and stewardship.
- Primary conversion: Support Reparations. Secondary: View Leadership.
- Search intent: Justice League GLM impact and accountability.
- Proposed title: `Impact and Accountability | Justice League GLM`
- Meta description: `Review dated Justice League GLM program evidence, its accountability framework, and separate supporter and donor acknowledgments.`
- H1: `Impact and Accountability`
- LCP candidate: Text H1.
- Schema: `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList`; no quantitative structured data.
- Canonical/indexability: `/impact/`; noindex until substantive, approved evidence exists.
- Internal links: Scholarship, Leadership, Reparations, Supporters, Donors, Support Reparations, and Contact.
