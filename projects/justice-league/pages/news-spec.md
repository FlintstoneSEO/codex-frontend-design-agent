# News and Stories Index Specification

- Route: `/news/`
- Page type: Editorial archive, Documentary Community mode
- Objective: Establish a dated editorial system and honest disconnected/empty states.
- Audience/intent: Supporters, media, partners, and researchers following current work.
- Primary conversion: Read a current story. Secondary: Subscribe.
- Search intent: Justice League GLM news and reparations news Lansing.
- Proposed title: `News and Stories | Justice League GLM`
- Meta description: `Review the editorial archive prototype for future dated Justice League GLM news, community stories, and updates.`
- H1: `News and Stories`
- LCP candidate: Text H1.
- Schema: `CollectionPage` only after content source approval; no schema for demo stories.
- Canonical/indexability: `/news/`; prototype noindex, intended index after source connection.
- Internal links: Demo story, Events, Reparations, Join, newsletter.

## Section hierarchy

| Order | Section | User question | Proof/content | Action |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Editorial status | Is this archive connected? | Wix Blog/CMS source unresolved | None |
| 2 | Story treatment | What will an entry contain? | Labelled demonstration headline, date role, excerpt, publisher | Open demo story |
| 3 | Empty/error behavior | What happens without records? | Honest state copy | Events |
| 4 | Subscription | How can I follow updates? | Non-submitting newsletter shell | Test shell |

## Requirements

- Media: No thumbnails until rights and captions are approved.
- Mobile: One-column editorial list with metadata before excerpt.
- Accessibility/performance: Article links are descriptive; newsletter errors are associated; demo switching uses minimal JS.
- Unknowns: `[NEEDS CLIENT INPUT: Confirm Wix Blog or CMS, migration inventory, authorship policy, media rights, and newsletter provider.]`
- Acceptance: Demo is visibly labelled; no invented story claim; empty/error states are keyboard reviewable.
