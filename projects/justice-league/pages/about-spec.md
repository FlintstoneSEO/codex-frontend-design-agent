# About Page Specification

- Route: `/about/`
- Page type: Organization overview, Documentary Community mode
- Objective: Explain the provisional mission, Greater Lansing focus, and organizational model without overstating proof.
- Audience/intent: Residents, prospective participants, donors, partners, and media evaluating purpose and legitimacy.
- Primary conversion: Join the Work. Secondary: Support Reparations.
- Search intent: Justice League of Greater Lansing mission and organization.
- Proposed title: `About the Justice League | Justice League GLM`
- Meta description: `Learn about the Justice League of Greater Lansing's provisional mission, local focus, and pathways for reparations work.`
- H1: `About the Justice League`
- LCP candidate: Text H1; no media until rights are approved.
- Schema: `AboutPage` only after organization copy is approved; prototype uses `WebPage` only.
- Canonical/indexability: `/about/`; prototype noindex, intended index after review.
- Internal links: History, Leadership, Reparations, Join, Support, Contact.

## Section hierarchy

| Order | Section | User question | Proof/content | Action |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local orientation | Who is this organization? | Approved mission placeholder and Greater Lansing context | Join the Work |
| 2 | Organizational model | How does the work connect? | Faith-rooted, community-centered positioning observed on the current site | Explore Reparations |
| 3 | Areas of work | What does it do? | Education, relationship building, scholarship, economic repair, collective action; labelled provisional | Scholarship or Events |
| 4 | Trust pathway | Where can I verify more? | Dated source notice and governance links | Leadership and Contact |

## Requirements

- Media: Text-led; no stock or synthetic people.
- Mobile: Preserve action-first order and convert ruled areas into one readable sequence.
- Accessibility/performance: Semantic sections and lists; no required client JavaScript; text is LCP.
- Unknowns: `[PLACEHOLDER: Approved organization mission]`; `[NEEDS CLIENT INPUT: Approve mission, service area, nonprofit language, and organizational description.]`
- Acceptance: One H1; visible provisional label; no unsupported status claim; all actions resolve.
