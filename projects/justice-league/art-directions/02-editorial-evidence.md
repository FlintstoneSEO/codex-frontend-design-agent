# Art Direction 02: The Case for Reparations

## Taxonomy family

Editorial Evidence

## Strategic concept

Build the site as a clear, evidence-led civic publication that connects history, local conditions, scholarships, policy, events, and action.

## Business rationale

The organization addresses a complex subject that requires explanation, credibility, and documented context. An editorial system can establish authority while still directing users toward donations, events, scholarships, and advocacy.

## Target emotional response

“This organization understands the issue, explains it responsibly, and gives me a credible way to act locally.”

## Visual-reference principles

- Investigative and civic editorial design
- Strong content hierarchy and annotated evidence
- Readable long-form pages
- Issue briefs, timelines, quotations, and source notes

Do not reproduce the recognizable composition of a newspaper, advocacy site, or publication.

## Color strategy

- Primarily light, paper-like canvases
- Dark ink-like text
- One strong brand action color
- Restrained secondary colors for categories, charts, and status
- No decorative gradients

## Typography strategy

- Distinctive editorial serif or slab-serif display face
- Neutral, highly legible sans serif for body and interface text
- Strong hierarchy without extreme headline sizes
- Numeric, date, source, and citation styles designed as part of the system

## Layout strategy

### Homepage composition

1. Editorial masthead with concise mission and a current-action rail
2. Lead issue or campaign presented as a feature story
3. “Why this matters in Greater Lansing” evidence section
4. Scholarship and program outcomes with verified data or narrative proof
5. Topic pathways such as history, education, scholarships, events, and action
6. Current events and announcements in a publication index
7. Leadership, governance, and support pathways

### Component grammar

- Strong rules and dividers
- Article indexes rather than promotional cards
- Pull quotes, source notes, timelines, fact panels, and definitions
- Side rails for related actions and resources
- Tables and charts only when they improve understanding
- Campaign CTA treated like a publication alert, not a floating pill

## Image direction

- Selective documentary images
- Historical documents or archival material where rights permit
- Annotated maps, timelines, scanned artifacts, and verified charts
- Images support evidence rather than fill space

## Motion strategy

- Functional disclosure for citations, timelines, filters, and related reading
- Minimal entrance animation
- No scroll-jacking, parallax, or decorative kinetic type

## Mobile behavior

- Article navigation becomes a clear table of contents
- Side rails move after the relevant section, not simply to the page bottom
- Long-form text maintains readable measure and heading rhythm
- Data tables receive accessible summaries or controlled horizontal regions
- Current action remains visible near the introduction without covering content

## Accessibility considerations

- Long-form pages need skip links, heading discipline, table-of-contents links, and readable line length
- Charts require text equivalents
- Source notes and footnotes must be keyboard and screen-reader accessible
- Historical documents require transcriptions where necessary
- Avoid overly small editorial metadata

## Performance considerations

- Lower image payload than the documentary direction
- Potential risk from interactive charts, document viewers, or embedded PDFs
- Prefer HTML content over embedded document viewers
- Load data visualizations only when they add decision value

## Risks

- May feel academic or institutional if community voices are underrepresented
- Requires disciplined writing and verified evidence
- Could make urgent participation feel secondary
- Content maintenance is more demanding than a simple campaign site

## Industry fit

Strong fit for advocacy, reparations education, scholarship transparency, and an organization seeking institutional credibility.

## Difference from alternatives

- More evidence- and reading-led than Lansing in Action
- More measured and institutional than Civic Momentum
- Uses editorial hierarchy, sources, and issue structure as the main design engine

## Fit score

- Strategic fit: 9/10
- Content feasibility: 6/10 pending evidence and content audit
- Accessibility risk: Moderate
- Performance risk: Low to moderate
- Distinctiveness potential: 8/10
