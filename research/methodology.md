# Research Methodology

## Research questions

1. Which design principles remain valid across industries?
2. Which conventions help users recognize and trust a business category?
3. Which conventions have become clichés or produce generic AI output?
4. How should conversion, SEO, accessibility, responsive behavior, and performance change design decisions before coding?
5. How can Codex load enough context to make strong decisions without receiving one enormous instruction file?

## Source hierarchy

1. Normative and official standards: W3C/WAI, Google Search Central, browser documentation
2. Research organizations: Nielsen Norman Group, Baymard Institute
3. Mature public design systems: GOV.UK and comparable systems
4. Real-world websites: national leaders, regional organizations, small businesses, distinctive brands
5. Galleries and platform showcases: used for discovery only, then validated against the actual website where possible

## Website sample protocol

Each industry includes ten examples and aims to contain:

- At least three national or established organizations
- At least three small or regional organizations
- At least two visually distinctive examples
- At least one strong mobile or conversion example
- A range of organization sizes and technical maturity

Examples were evaluated for business type, target audience, primary conversion, visual direction, typography, color, photography, layout, hierarchy, navigation, trust, mobile behavior, accessibility risk, performance risk, SEO structure, transferable lessons, and patterns not to copy.

## Evidence labels

- **Sourced guidance**: directly supported by a listed source.
- **Observed pattern**: visible in one or more reviewed sites, but not automatically a best practice.
- **Professional inference**: synthesized recommendation based on standards, research, and comparative analysis.
- **Project-specific decision**: must be justified against the project brief.

## Limitations

This Phase 1 package is a design-intelligence baseline, not a claim that every sampled page was exhaustively tested with assistive technology, field Core Web Vitals, analytics, or private conversion data. Visual and structural observations must be validated again when examples change. Automated tests cannot replace keyboard, screen-reader, content, or visual review.
