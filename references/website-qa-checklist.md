# Website QA Checklist

## Content
- [ ] Verified facts and placeholders resolved
- [ ] Links, downloads, phone, email, forms, and confirmation states work
- [ ] Empty, error, loading, and success states reviewed

## Visual and responsive
- [ ] Screenshot review completed at required widths
- [ ] No clipping, overlap, overflow, broken crop, or orphan heading
- [ ] Brand and industry fit pass rubric

## Accessibility
- [ ] Automated scan plus keyboard and screen-reader smoke tests
- [ ] Forms and dialogs tested
- [ ] Reduced motion and zoom tested

## SEO
- [ ] Metadata, headings, canonical, robots, sitemap, schema, status codes
- [ ] Internal links and social previews checked

## Performance
- [ ] Budgets and representative Lighthouse runs
- [ ] LCP, CLS, long tasks, images, fonts, and third parties reviewed

## Browser/device
- [ ] Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari
- [ ] iOS Safari and Android Chrome representative test
